Grid and guides

Selections snap to the canvas center and edges, plus any grid or thirds lines you can see. Hold Option or Alt while dragging to move freely. Arrow keys nudge a selection, Shift for bigger steps.

Your projects

Your work stays on this device

Mocean saves your projects in this browser, on this device. Nothing is uploaded, so if you clear your browser data or switch devices, your projects won't come with you.

To keep a backup, use Export, then Save project. You can open that file again any time.

On iPhone and iPad, Safari can clear a site's saved data if you don't visit it for a while. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open Mocean from there to keep your projects safe.

Add picture
